New in this version:

- **Settings in seven pages**, each starting with a line on what it is for: General, Hotkeys, Dictation (with the panel shown while you speak), Meetings (with calls), Engines and models, History and privacy, Appearance.
- **Auto, Offer or Off for each call app.** Teams, Zoom and Google Meet each start a transcript on their own, offer one in the panel, or do nothing. **Rules by meeting name** decide for some meetings instead, for example *Teams, contains "Daily scrum", then Auto*. Older settings files keep working until you change Calls.
- **Every model in Engines and models**, the voice and speaker detection models included, grouped by kind, each saying what it is for and what uses it, or that nothing does and it can be deleted.
- **Home is a status page:** what needs attention (with a button for each problem), the dictation status, the meeting, your last 10 dictations and your last 10 meetings, a click opening one.
- **The 🎙 menu in groups:** meeting, dictation and Shepit, with a language submenu for meetings and one for dictation.
- **One period menu** in Dictations, Statistics and People, with Custom… in all three.
- **Settings you can see are editable:** text fields have a border and a Save button, numbers are picked from menus, and Clear history… and Clear statistics… ask in a sheet on the window. The Add word dialog is in English.
