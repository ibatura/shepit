New in this version:

- **Shepit has its own icon.** A rhombus of bars, black at its edges and in its heart, red between, on linen: a sound wave drawn as the oldest motif of Ukrainian embroidery. It shows in Finder, the Dock and ⌘Tab while the window is open, About Shepit, Spotlight and Privacy & Security, with macOS 26's dark, clear and tinted looks. It needs Xcode to build: with only the Command Line Tools, Shepit builds as before, with the generic icon.
- **The menu bar shows the rhombus instead of emoji.** Red while recording, faded while transcribing, an orange dot for a problem, and a red dot in front while a meeting transcript runs. It follows a light or dark menu bar, and VoiceOver reads the status on it.
