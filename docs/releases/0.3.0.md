New in this version:

- **Back and forward** in the window: ‹ and › at the top, or ⌘[ and ⌘], go back and forward through the sections and the meeting or person you chose in them, as in a browser. A hint on each arrow says where it goes. A meeting or person that is gone since is skipped.
- **The sidebar collapses to its icons:** the sidebar button next to the arrows, or ⌃⌘S, switches between the full sidebar and a narrow column of icons, each with its name as a hint. A running transcript shows as a red dot there. Shepit remembers which you chose.
