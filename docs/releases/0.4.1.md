New in this version:

- **Faster as meetings add up.** The meetings list, Home's recent meetings and every read of one person in People find their rows through an index instead of reading every row. Delete voice… on a year of meetings takes 5 ms instead of 80. `meetings.sqlite` becomes version 3 the first time this version opens it; older versions of Shepit still read it.
- **Dictation hotkeys are never held up by voices.** May also be in, in People, and the forgetting of old unnamed voices after each meeting now read off the main thread.
- **A change in People or Meetings no longer fails with "database is locked"** when it meets the forgetting of old voices: it waits for it.
- The words of meetings listed before version 0.2.0 are no longer counted from their notes; such meetings show "–".
